Exact comparison scope

Comparisons preserve dataset order, dtype, shape and floating-point bits, and check scientific attribute inventories and values. Compression and chunk layout are outside this scientific-value check. Whole-file hashes can therefore differ when excluded timing fields differ.

Inspiral excludes exactly four timing/rate datasets: H1/search/run_time, filter_rate_per_core, setup_time_fraction and templates_per_core within the same search group. Search start/end times and gating values remain checked. Live excludes no datasets: the expected executable is verified before normalizing its name and the output destination in command_line; every other argument and attribute remains checked. No extra scientific exclusions or fitted tolerances were introduced. Profiled timings are excluded from benchmark samples.

Bounded original-route agreement

The first two physically distinct bank rows retain IDs 0 and 1, their exact parameters, header and compressed waveform arrays. Both native original CPU and current JAX with all 298 registered original selectors selected complete the same five segments and 1,904 valid H1 seconds and save five triggers. Their 18 scientific datasets match exactly; both attribute inventories are empty. All 25 source/input/argument/workload contract checks pass. Selecting every registered control does not imply this search exercises every registered API.

The current CLI's device_linear option remains present, but the selected decompress reference control overrides that calculation with its original route. Original CPU and JAX execution/batch/reference options differ explicitly; their science arguments otherwise agree. The JAX signal dtype is observed as complex64; native CPU intermediate signal dtype is not independently captured.

This proves composed original-route agreement for these two templates and saved outputs only. The full 1,536-template all-original attempt was stopped with its first 64-template batch still at segment 2 of 5 after approximately 11 minutes. It remains incomplete and supplies no full-bank agreement result.

Live ordering and timing limits

Live uses 1,024 distinct TaylorF2 templates, two MPI ranks and 2,048 Hz sampling. The worker reports complex64 arrays. Its 33 blocks include 26 valid eight-second blocks for each detector: 208 valid H1 seconds and 208 valid L1 seconds. Source, input, argument and workload contracts pass for all samples and the profile.

The sample-3 strict failure is foreground/type: H1-L1 becomes L1-H1 in one foreground file. All 1,014 numeric or structured datasets, the other 52 string datasets and all compared attributes remain exact. Detector membership is the same. Source tracing shows the native event manager constructs a detector set, whose iteration order passes through combinations into the original/JAX label join. Isolated hash-seed controls reproduce both labels, and the pristine native source shares this construction. The failure remains recorded; no dataset was excluded and no production fix was applied.

All recorded Live zero_warm_compilation flags are false. Compilation-free steady-state timing is therefore uncertified; those flags alone do not prove uncached compilation. No fresh pristine-CPU complete-search comparison is available for this Live workload.

Full conditioned-strain arrays, complete PSD comparison coverage and early/later decision audits are missing. Full-bank composed reference agreement is also unestablished. Scientific qualification and qualified capacity/speedup claims remain unavailable; diagnostic elapsed measurements do not relax these gates.
